Your ward's problems. Solved on paper, ready to act.

This is not a complaint box. JanConnect turns citizen reports into a ready-to-use brief — department named, steps numbered, cost in ₹, timeline in days. The corporator's office decides. We keep the brief data-driven and the loop visible.

How it works
1.Official PMC social posts and Google News RSS are classified into civic news and matched to Pune wards
2.AI clusters similar reports by ward and issue type
3.A step-by-step solution is generated with department, cost & timeline
4.Ward office reviews and acts → citizens see the resolution on this very map

About JanConnect

JanConnect groups public PMC Facebook/Instagram posts and Google News RSS articles by ward and issue type, then turns them into a ready-to-use action brief for the ward office.

The corporator's office decides. We keep the brief data-driven and the loop visible.
